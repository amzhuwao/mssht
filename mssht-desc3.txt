# Financial Module for a Tertiary Institution School Management System

## Full Features and Functional Specifications
the finance module must support flexible billing structures, multiple payment models, installment plans, sponsorships, corporate clients, and audit-compliant accounting.

---

# 1. PURPOSE OF THE FINANCIAL MODULE

The financial module should manage:

* Student billing
* Revenue collection
* Invoicing
* Accounting
* Budgeting
* Procurement
* Financial reporting
* Debt management
* Asset tracking
* Compliance and auditing

It should operate like a mini ERP system.

---

# 2. CORE COMPONENTS OF THE FINANCIAL MODULE

The module should contain the following submodules:

1. Student Billing & Invoicing
2. Payment Management
3. Accounts Receivable
4. Accounts Payable
5. General Ledger
6. Budgeting & Forecasting
7. Payroll Integration
8. Procurement & Purchasing
9. Asset Management
10. Sponsorship & Corporate Billing
11. Ancillary Billing
12. Financial Reporting & Analytics
13. Banking & Reconciliation
14. Audit & Compliance
15. Multi-Currency
16. Financial Security & Controls

---

---

## Features

### 3.1 Fee Structure Management

* Tuition fees
* Registration fees
* Examination fees

* Library fines
* Penalty charges

### 3.2 Flexible Program Billing

Must support:

| Program Type              | Billing Model    |
| ------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Short courses             | Once-off payment |
| Professional certificates | Module-based     |
| Diplomas/HNDs             | Semester-based   |
| Corporate training        | Group invoicing  |

---

### 3.3 Automatic Invoice Generation

System automatically generates:

* Student invoices
* Receipts
* Statements
* Credit notes
* Debit notes

---

### 3.4 Installment Plans

Supports:

* Monthly payment plans
* Custom schedules
* Partial payments
* Down payments

---

### 3.5 Dynamic Fee Rules

Example:

* International students pay different fees
* Returning students retain old pricing
* Discounts for early payment

### 3.6 Bulk Billing

Used for:

* Corporate-sponsored students

* Group registrations

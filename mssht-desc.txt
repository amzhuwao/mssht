Manica Skyview School of Hospitality and Tourism School Management System
Features and Specifications for Short Courses, Professional Certificates, and Higher National Diploma Institutions
TERTIARY INSTITUTION SCHOOL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
Features and Specifications for a Short Courses, Professional Certificates, and Higher National Diploma Institution
1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW
The system is a centralized web-based and mobile-enabled platform designed to manage academic operations, student lifecycle, finance and billing, course delivery, assessments and examinations, human resources, communication, reporting and analytics, and compliance requirements.
2. USER ROLES AND ACCESS LEVELS

•	- Registrar / Academic Administrator
•	- Finance Officer
•	- Lecturer / Trainer
•	- Student
•	- HOD / Dean
•	- Librarian
•	- External Examiner / Accrediting Body

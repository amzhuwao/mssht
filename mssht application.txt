GUIDE TO APPLICANTS

1) All applicants must complete all sections of the application form carefully and legally.

2) Applicants should submit this form to the Administrator, 77 Second Street Mutare, Zimbabwe

3) Forms downloaded from the Internet should be submitted with an application fee of us$10.00.

4) All applicants MUST submit with this form, photocopies (NOT ORIGINALS) of all relevant academic and or Professional certificates referred to in the application including birth certificate and ID card. The copies of certificates must be certified by a Commissioner of Oaths or Head/Principal of the institution at which the examinations were taken.

5) Applicants must give careful thought to their choice of programme in relation to the entry requirements for that programme and their own subject passes. Applicants who are in doubt regarding the choice of programme should seek advice from the Administrator before completing their forms.

6) It is also important for applicants to understand that admission will be made, as far as possible, in accordance with the programme preference made by the applicant. THEREFORE, FIRST PREFERENCE SHOULD BE WHAT THE CANDIDATE GENUINELY WANTS TO PURSUE. No request(s) for change of programme(s) will be entertained after the initial registration exercise.

7) Unsuccessful applications will be due to:

i) Programmes being full

ii) Failure to meet the requirements in terms of passes or entrance requirements

iii) Late submission of academic results

8) Applicants must ensure that they have the necessary finance to pay the full fees on registration day. No students will be allowed to register at the school unless they have the necessary fees or proof of sponsorship.
